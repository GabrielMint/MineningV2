# Minening — Art Style Guide

Source of truth: [docs/GDD.md](../docs/GDD.md) §6–8 (including the direction revisions in §7).
Claude reads this before making any art.

**The game is hand-drawn vector art made by code** (next section): base, UI, talent tree and
both mine floors. The pixel-art sections after it describe the earlier look and stay as reference.

## Base: hand-drawn, by code
Source: `art/hand/*.hd.js` (`base`, `characters`; `_lib.hd.js` only includes the palette),
built with the **HandDrawn** tool at `C:\Users\Gabriel Mint\Documents\Projects\Tools\HandDrawn`
(its README has the whole API and the shape kit). Output: SVG, exported to `assets/hand/`.
Nothing is generated by AI; every shape is written in the art file and every random choice is
seeded, so a rebuild gives the same drawing.
- **Units:** art is drawn at 4× the logical pixels (a 20×24 logical barrel is ~84×104 units).
  `scripts/world/hand.gd` rasterizes each SVG at the window's exact scale, so it is sharp at
  1:1 at any window size. Characters use 128×128 frames (feet on y = 124).
- **Ink:** dark `k` outlines (`d.ink`), thicker on the bottom-right (light from the top-left),
  thinner inner lines in `d`/`D`. Small parts get thinner ink (1–2 units), big shapes 2.4–2.8.
- **Fills:** flat colors from the Endesga keys, one lit area toward the top-left, the shadow
  side **hatched by hand** (`d.hatch`), never gradients. Anti-aliased edges are fine here.
- **Wobble:** every outline wobbles a little (`wobble` 0.4–1 on props, more on terrain), so
  nothing is ruler-straight. Characters reseed each part (`d.seed`) so lines don't jitter
  between animation frames.
- **Story marks, not noise:** wood grain, knots and nails; cracks on rocks; pebbles and cart
  ruts on the path; grass as tufts of tapered blades, gathered in patches with blade fringes.
- **Ground:** one painting for the whole base (`ground` asset); the path layout lives there.
- Shadows are white masks (`shadow_s/m/l`) colored in Godot like the pixel ones.
- Preview: `node <HandDrawn>/bin/hd.js build art/hand/<file>.hd.js --out art/out/hand/<file> --png 1 --preview 2`
  (1× PNGs + zoomed `_preview/`). The `hand-artist` agent knows this workflow.

## Look (mines, pixel art)
References live in `references/` at the project root (Godot imports them; they are not game art).
**Top-down pixel art in the spirit of Enter the Gungeon** (`enter_the_gungeon_topdown_style.jpg`):
- **Hard 1px outlines** in `k` (`outline: "k"`) on every sprite: characters, enemies, ores,
  props, drops. Tiles have no outline.
- **Dark, low-contrast floors** (f1 `D` dirt, f2 `n` stone) so outlined sprites pop; walls
  have a lit top lip and a black void above.
- Flat colors, 2–3 shades per part, light from the top-left; depth through a lit top/front face
  and a cast shadow under every standing object.
- **Textured surfaces with a reason**: dirt has pebbles, rock has slabs and seams, wood has
  grain and nails. Sparse, never filler noise.

**Characters** (`enter_the_gungeon_characters_reference.png`): chibi, the head is about half the
height (helmet/hat + face ~15 px), stubby body (~10 px), tiny legs (~5 px), dot eyes (1×2 `k`),
tiny mouth.

**Ore nodes** (`dont_starve_mining_reference.webp`): a bit **bigger than the miner**, never
smaller. Angular boulders of 2–3 stacked slabs (`oreRock` in `_lib.px.js`), the ore growing in
the seams between slabs.

## Scale — no mixels, ever
- **Viewport:** 480×270, scaled ×4 to 1920×1080 (integer scale only).
- **Player:** 32×32 frame (30×30 + outline), ~22 px wide, big head. Everything is
  sized against the player: tiles 32×32 · rats ~28×14 · golems ~40×34 · ore nodes 44×38 ·
  drops ~10–12 px · props 32–96 px · UI 9-slice pieces 24×24 (8px margins), icons 24×24,
  talent nodes 40×40. Text uses the 5×7 pixel font.
- Use the extra resolution for detail: eyes with highlights, cloth folds, wood grain, rock
  facets — not just bigger flat shapes.
- One art pixel = one game pixel. Never scale or rotate sprites in-engine; pre-draw rotations
  (the mining swing has 8 directions). Button tweens move by whole pixels, never `scale`.

## Color — exclusively Endesga 32
Keys in `art/src/palette.px.js`. No other color may appear.

| Family | Ramp (dark → light) | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Stone / metal | `k K n s S z w` | rock, iron, cool shadows |
| Earth / wood | `k d D e E c` | mine floor, timber, leather, parchment UI |
| Skin | `d D p P` | faces, hands |
| Foliage | `k j h g G` | grass, moss, bushes |
| Water / crystal | `k K b B C` | water, sapphire, crystal cave |
| Red | `d r R x` | damage, rat eyes |
| Fire / gold | `d o O f Y y` | lamps, gold, helmets, highlights |
| Purple | `d v V q` | toxic gas, magic |

- Light comes from the **top-left**. Cast shadows fall down-right.
- No anti-aliasing against transparency; no semi-transparent pixels in sprites.

## Effects in Godot (stay inside the palette)
- No `modulate` tints on art, no alpha fades, no `CanvasModulate`, no Light2D.
- **Exception — white masks:** a pure-white sprite (font glyphs, shadow ellipses) may be colored
  with `self_modulate`/`font_color` set **exactly** to a palette color; the result is that color.
- **Hit flash:** shader that paints every opaque pixel one palette color for 1–2 frames.
- **Cast shadows:** white ellipse mask colored with the floor's darker shade (per biome).
- **Fades:** dither or step through palette colors, not alpha.

## Characters and animation
- Movement is free (8-way and analog); sprites show 4 directions: `down`, `up`, `left`
  (`right` = `left` flipped).
- Tags `action_direction`: `idle_down`, `walk_left`, ... 8 fps walk, blink on idle.

## Places
- **Base — A Entrada da Mina:** sunny grass clearing, dirt path, grey cliff with a timbered
  mine entrance, the merchant's stall, barrels, crates, lantern, bushes.
- **Floor 1 — A Antiga Mina:** warm brown dirt floor with pebbles; brown rock walls of stacked
  boulders; water seeping; broken timber supports; old miners' skeletons. Ore: **iron**
  (grey rock with silvery and rust flecks). Enemy: **rats**.
- **Floor 2 — Caverna de Cristal:** pale blue-grey floor, deep blue crystal walls with cyan
  highlights, crystal shards. Ore: **sapphire**. Enemy: **crystal golems**.

## UI
Stardew-like: warm wood frames (`D`/`e` with a `E` bevel) around cream parchment (`c`),
dark text (`d`/`D`) on parchment, light text (`c`/`w`) on dark. Talent tree: dark background,
round node frames with 16×16 icons, links drawn as 1px lines.
